Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. Summary

CatEducation Uploader ("the Tool") is a personal tool used by one creator for their own TikTok account. It does not collect data from any other person.

2. Data the Tool handles

  • The operator's TikTok authorization tokens and basic profile information (display name), received through TikTok Login Kit with the user.info.basic scope.
  • Video files that the operator chooses to upload, sent to TikTok through the Content Posting API with the video.upload scope.

3. Where data is stored

Tokens and files are stored only on the operator's own computer. They are not sent to any server other than TikTok, are not shared with third parties, and are not sold or used for advertising.

4. Retention and deletion

The operator can delete the stored tokens at any time and can revoke the Tool's access in TikTok under the account's security and permissions settings.

5. Children

The Tool is not directed at children and is not offered to the public.

6. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version. Contact via GitHub: github.com/mpik
