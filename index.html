CatEducation Uploader

CatEducation Uploader is a personal tool used by one creator to upload their own original, educational cat videos to their own TikTok account as drafts.

After a one-time login with TikTok, the tool sends a finished video file from the creator's computer to the TikTok inbox as a draft. The creator then reviews the draft inside TikTok, adds the caption and the AI-generated content label, and publishes it manually. The tool never posts publicly by itself.

The tool is not offered to third parties and does not access data of any other TikTok user.
